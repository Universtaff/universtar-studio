Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy covers the internal publishing tool operated by Universtar Studio ("we", "us") at universtaff.github.io/universtar-studio.

Who uses this tool

The tool is private. It is used only by Universtar Studio staff to manage TikTok and YouTube accounts that we own. There is no public registration and we do not onboard third-party users.

What we access

After an account owner grants authorization, we access, through the official TikTok APIs and only for the authorizing account:

  • Basic account profile (display name, avatar, open ID)
  • Video metadata and performance metrics for videos on that account
  • Comments left on videos on that account, in order to read and reply
  • Permission to upload and publish video content to that account

We do not access private messages, follower personal data, payment information, or any account we do not own.

What we store

We store the OAuth access token and refresh token for each authorized account, plus video metadata and aggregate metrics used for scheduling and reporting. Tokens are held on our own machines in files that are not committed to version control and not shared with anyone.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through the TikTok APIs with third parties. We do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not combine it with data from other sources to identify individuals.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or when an account is no longer managed. Metrics are kept only as long as they are useful for scheduling, and are deleted on request. An account owner can revoke our access at any time from TikTok account settings, which immediately invalidates our tokens.

Commenters and viewers

Comments we read are already public on TikTok. We use them only to reply from the account that received them. We do not export, store long-term, or analyse commenter identities.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new date above.
